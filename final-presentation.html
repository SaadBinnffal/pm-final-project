Problem, value and hypothesis

Restore frontline usefulness while preserving enterprise capabilities.

Business risk

Declining frontline usage and the largest enterprise account's competitor trial threaten renewal confidence.

Problem hook

Protect RouteLogic's largest account by addressing administrative friction that slows frontline fleet coordination.

Value proposition

Make urgent coordinator tasks simpler and faster while preserving the advanced capabilities enterprise users need.

First bet

Reduce friction in required compliance reviews for the existing coordinator persona.

M3 classroom evidenceInterpretation

Hypothesis

Guided Compliance Review will reduce mean correctly completed task time by at least 30% versus concurrent control over four weeks, while protecting task completion, record validity, and Core Dispatch CSAT.

Planning reference: 14.6 to 10.22 minutes. These are a classroom baseline and proposed target, not an achieved result. Handoff completion, 31% of all starts with a proposed 40% goal, remains secondary.

Roadmap, PRD and prototype

Scope: 2 engineers, 1 designer, 1 CS lead. Proposed four-week pilot across three accounts. B1 effort assumes reusable, trustworthy records and existing validation/save capability.

PRD and prototype

  1. Daily Overview: find or resume the required review.
  2. Compliance Review: inspect known context, confirm checks, record an outcome or exception.
  3. Confirmation: inspect the saved record and distinguish clear completion from follow-up.

Required behavior includes validation, explicit human review, preserved drafts after save failures, and truthful exception and unsaved-change states.

A decision rule for the workflow package, with explicit feasibility limits.

ParameterProposed decision
MethodStable 50/50 A/B assignment at coordinator level within accounts, if independence and account permissions permit.
A / BExisting compliance workflow / bounded B1 review package including B5. Hold other operational behavior and training policy constant.
PrimaryEqual-weighted coordinator-level mean clean-completion time. Goal: at least 30% relative reduction versus concurrent A.
Sample42 evaluable independent coordinators per arm, conditional on an assumed 7-minute SD. Actual variance, enrollment, and design remain unverified.
TimingProposed 9 Nov–6 Dec 2026. Fixed read: 8 Dec, 12:00 Asia/Riyadh.
GuardrailsCore Dispatch CSAT at least 4.1/5 and not below A. No quality or completion deterioration. Pause for critical false-completion or validation defects.

The binary-rate builder calculator does not apply to minutes. The continuous-outcome calculation needs verified variance and independent users. Three accounts do not establish 84 coordinators. Repeated tasks are not independent participants.

Individual insights

The judgments that changed the project.

Friction

Keeping the story coherent when M3 shifted the first test toward compliance. I preserved M2's original evidence and avoided inventing compliance interview findings.

The statistical challenge was matching a time metric to a feasible independent sample rather than treating a percentage calculator as universal.

Aha moment

Simplification still has to preserve the truth of the operational task.

The prototype's unsaved-edit gap showed why fast completion means little if a user cannot trust what has actually saved.

What I will carry forward

  • Read usage together with satisfaction, task time, and role context.
  • Protect scope around one evidence-backed user problem.
  • Audit AI suggestions and test unhappy paths.
  • Define decision rules and adoption support before launch.
